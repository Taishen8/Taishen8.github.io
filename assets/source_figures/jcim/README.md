## jcim_main_fig_02.png
- Source: `JCIM.docx`
- Original media: `media/image2.png`
- Size: 583 x 455 px
- File: `portfolio_site/assets/source_figures/jcim/jcim_main_fig_02.png`
- Nearby caption: Figure 2.Violin plot comparing HDOCK scores across peptides grouped by their CAMP consensus score. The CAMP consensus score reflects the maximum number of 5-fold cross-validation instances where a mutant’s predicted binding affinity exceeded the reference peptide’s affinity for either hHsp90-NTD or dHsp90-NTD. For example, a score of "4" indicates the mutant outperformed the wild type in 4/5 folds for at least one Hsp90 isoform.

## jcim_main_fig_08.png
- Source: `JCIM.docx`
- Original media: `media/image8.png`
- Size: 1270 x 556 px
- File: `portfolio_site/assets/source_figures/jcim/jcim_main_fig_08.png`
- Nearby caption: Figure 8. The width of the ATP binding pocket is a critical factor determining the effective binding of peptides and their mutants to the protein. (a) A cross-sectional view of the Hsp90-NTD protein. The residues at the entrance of the ATP binding pocket are predominantly polar (K58 and K112), while the bottom is mainly hydrophobic except for D93. (b) Distribution of ATP-binding pocket entrance widths across different Hsp90-NTD/peptide complexes, with corresponding median values indicated. The inset illustrates a significant positive correlation between calculated binding free energies and the entrance width.
