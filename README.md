# Haipeng Gan

I am an MSc student in chemistry at Nanjing University, working on AI-assisted molecular discovery.

My research has moved from peptide inhibitor screening, to reaction-language-model training, to AI-assisted discovery of catalytic self-assembling peptides. Across these projects, I became interested in a central question: how can AI systems make reliable discovery decisions when literature, simulations, learned predictors, and experiments are all incomplete?

This website is a compact visual appendix for my PhD applications. It includes:

- project summaries for SAPIENS, JCIM peptide inhibitor design, and ChemBART;
- selected workflow figures and manuscript-derived visual evidence;
- my CV and TOEFL score report;
- a short research-direction statement on evidence-grounded and feedback-driven discovery.

My long-term goal is to build scientific agents and modeling workflows that can reason over uncertain evidence, choose useful tools, and prioritize experimentally testable candidates in molecular and materials discovery.
